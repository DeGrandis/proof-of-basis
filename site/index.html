How it works

  1. Dump in your exchange history. CSV exports from Coinbase, River, Strike and Fold, or a spreadsheet of your own. It recognizes each one.
  2. Connect your real wallets. Paste an xpub or output descriptor. It reads them from your own node's Electrum server, so your addresses never touch anyone else's.
  3. It untangles everything. Withdrawals matched to deposits, coins followed from wallet to wallet, every sale traced to its purchase. What it can't explain waits for a label.
  4. Hand your accountant one package. Form 8949 lines, your 1099-DA forms checked against your records, where every coin came from, and your proof.

Features

Track every coin

Buys on an exchange, withdrawals to cold storage, spends from a hot wallet, sales back on an exchange: linked up, so every sale knows which purchase it came from.

Ready for tax season*

Five guided steps from "is everything here?" to one download for your tax professional, with each exchange's 1099-DA checked against your own records.

Ask your own AI

An optional assistant on your own Ollama looks things up, charts them and proposes fixes you apply with one tap. Every change can be undone.

Private by design

One file on your own machine. It talks to your node, never phones home, and the accountant package leaves out addresses unless you ask.

One click on your node

Install it from the Umbrel community store or as a StartOS package, and it finds your node's Electrum server on its own.

* Untangling and tracing work wherever you live. The tax side (Form 8949, 1099-DA checks, the accountant package) follows United States rules, in US dollars.

Get started

Pick where you run it. Each install asks you to set a password the first time you open it.

  1. On your Umbrel, open the App Store, then ⋯ → Community App Stores.
  2. Paste this address and add it:
    https://github.com/DeGrandis/proof-of-basis-umbrel
  3. Open the new store and install Proof of Basis. It needs Electrs or Fulcrum on the Umbrel, and connects to it by itself.
  4. For the assistant, install Ollama from the App Store, then in Proof of Basis open Settings → Assistant and enter:
    http://ollama_ollama_1:11434
  1. Download the package for your server from the latest release: x86_64 for Intel or AMD, aarch64 for ARM.
  2. In StartOS, click Sideload in the top bar and choose the file.
  3. Start Proof of Basis and open its Web UI. Fulcrum or Electrs, and Ollama if you have it, connect on their own.

For StartOS 0.4. The Node Services and Reset Password actions are on the service's page.

  1. Run the image (amd64 or arm64):
    docker run -d --name proof-of-basis -p 8080:8080 -v pob-data:/data --restart unless-stopped ghcr.io/degrandis/proof-of-basis:latest
  2. Open http://<this computer>:8080 and set a password.
  3. In Settings, enter your Fulcrum or Electrs server, then add your wallets and drop in your exchange exports.

Or build it yourself with docker compose up -d --build from the repository.
