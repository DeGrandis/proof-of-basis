        Prices

        Only goes online when you press Update. Updating prices is the only time this app contacts anything besides your node. The image already includes daily history up to when it was built.

        Lightning node (optional)

        Create a read-only rune: lightning-cli createrune restrictions='[["method=getinfo","method=bkpr-listincome","method=bkpr-listbalances"]]' Stored here, never shown again and never logged. The app only reads from your node, and only when you sync.

        Security

        Signs out other devices. Forgot it? On the server, run docker compose exec btctax /btctax reset-password and set a new one on the next visit. Over plain http the password isn't encrypted in transit; use HTTPS (a reverse proxy or Tailscale) to reach this app from outside your home.

        Assistant (optional)

        In the demo, the assistant runs on a hosted model. Your own copy uses a model on your own Ollama server.

        Everything you ask, and everything the assistant looks up to answer you, goes to this provider.

        Chat using your own Ollama. Pick a tool-capable model. Adds a chat button to every page. Your data goes only to that address, never anywhere else. Models that use tools (for example qwen3:8b or llama3.1:8b) can look things up. If Ollama runs on this same computer, use http://host.docker.internal:11434, since inside Docker "localhost" is the app itself. More memory lets it consider more at once but needs more RAM on the Ollama machine. Chats are saved here, included in backups and removed by Wipe.
        Works with any provider that speaks the OpenAI chat format (for example OpenRouter, Mistral or Groq), or with LM Studio or llama.cpp on your own network. Pick a model that can call tools. The key is saved on this server and never shown again. Each question sends the provider a summary of your holdings and the records the assistant looks up. The provider's own terms decide what it keeps. Chats are still saved here, included in backups and removed by Wipe.

        Backup and restore

        Download a backup
        Everything, including xpubs. Keep it encrypted. Settings, wallets and their keys, imports, labels, protected purchases, scans, prices and your Lightning node's history and rune. There's no way to recover a forgotten passphrase.
        Restore from a backup
        Replaces everything; current data saved first.

        Advanced backup (optional)

        A path inside the app. To use a share on your network, mount it there. With Docker Compose, add a volume such as - /mnt/nas/proof-of-basis:/backup (a share the host has mounted), or a named volume with the nfs or cifs driver. Without a mount, the folder doesn't exist and saving says so, rather than writing inside the container where a rebuild would lose it.
        Always encrypted. A check uploads only if your data changed since the newest backup there. Each check compares a fingerprint (SHA-256) of your data with the one in the newest backup's name, so an unchanged day costs one listing and no upload. The keys and passphrase are saved on this server, never shown again, and left out of backups. There's no way to recover a forgotten passphrase. To restore, download a backup below and use Restore from a backup.

        Before you start

        Send your data to a hosted provider?

        With a hosted provider, the assistant runs on another company's computers. That company can read everything you type in the chat and everything the assistant sends it to answer you:

        • a summary of your holdings, with every question
        • the records it looks up: amounts, dates, prices, account and wallet names, transaction IDs and addresses

        Together these can show how much bitcoin you have and where it is. This app doesn't remove any of it before sending, and it can't control what the provider keeps, logs, learns from or hands over. Once something is sent, it can't be taken back.

        Your records themselves stay on this server. With Ollama on your own network, nothing leaves it.

        I accept my data leaking to a provider
