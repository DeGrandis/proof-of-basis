Tax season

Five steps to gather what one tax year needs, check it, and hand it to your tax professional. Each step shows what's done; open any of them to change things, right up to the end.

  1. Every account imported or scanned through the end of the year, nothing from the year waiting in Review, and every coin sold has a known purchase. Exchange files can be imported any time on Imports.

    • Each exchange sends a Form 1099-DA (a PDF) early in the year. Drop each one on its exchange below and type its total proceeds: the form goes into your package, and the total is checked against your records.

        Your node

        Tax

        The IRS treats BTC spent on a network fee as a disposal, even when moving coins between your own wallets (FAQs 81 and 97). Filing status sets how much net capital loss a year can deduct ($3,000, or $1,500 married filing separately); the rest carries forward. Tax years and holding periods follow your time zone's calendar.

        Prices

        Updating prices is the only time this app contacts anything besides your node, and only when you press the button. The image already includes daily history up to when it was built.

        Lightning node (optional)

        Make a rune that can only read the bookkeeper, on the node: lightning-cli createrune restrictions='[["method=getinfo","method=bkpr-listincome","method=bkpr-listbalances"]]'. It's stored here, never shown again and never logged. The app only reads from your node, and only when you sync.

        Security

        Changing the password signs out every other device. Forgot it? On the server, run docker compose exec btctax /btctax reset-password and set a new one on the next visit. Over plain http on your network the password isn't encrypted in transit; use HTTPS (for example a reverse proxy or Tailscale) to reach this app from outside your home.

        Assistant (optional)

        Adds a chat button to every page that answers questions about your data, using a model on your own Ollama server. Your data goes only to that address, never anywhere else. Pick a model that can use tools (for example qwen3:8b or llama3.1:8b) so it can look things up. If Ollama runs on this same computer, use http://host.docker.internal:11434, since inside Docker "localhost" is the app itself. More memory lets it consider more at once but needs more RAM on the Ollama machine. Chats are saved here, included in backups and removed by Wipe.

        Backup and restore

        Download a backup
        One file with everything: settings, wallets and their keys, imports, labels, protected purchases, scans, prices and your Lightning node's history and rune. It holds your xpubs, so keep encryption on. There's no way to recover a forgotten passphrase.
        Restore from a backup
        Restoring replaces everything in this app with the backup's contents. A copy of the current database is saved beside it first.

        Recheck everything

        Rescans every wallet with your Fulcrum server, then redoes all matching and tax figures. Nothing is deleted: imports, labels and protected purchases stay as they are.

        Delete all data

        Deletes every wallet, import, label, price and setting from this app's database and compacts the file. Use it to start over, or when you are done testing.
